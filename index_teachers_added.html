Welcome to Lakshya Convent School

At Lakshya Convent School, we believe that every child is unique and deserves the best start in life. Established with a vision to provide quality education rooted in strong values, we have been nurturing young minds from Play Group to Class VIII.

Our school follows the CBSE pattern curriculum, delivered by highly qualified and experienced teachers who are passionate about shaping the future generation. We combine traditional values with modern teaching methodologies to create a holistic learning experience.

With state-of-the-art facilities, a safe and secure campus, and a child-friendly environment, Lakshya Convent School is the perfect place for your child to learn, grow, and succeed.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our school

What is the admission process? +

Admission is open for Play Group to Class VIII. Parents need to fill the online admission form or visit the school office. Documents required include birth certificate, Aadhar card, passport size photos, and previous school TC (if applicable). An interaction session may be conducted.

What is the fee structure? +

Our fee structure is competitive and affordable. We offer various payment options including monthly, quarterly, and annual payments. Scholarships are available for meritorious students. Please contact the school office or fill the inquiry form for detailed fee information.

Is school transport available? +

Yes, we provide safe and reliable school bus services covering all major areas of Nawada and nearby villages. All buses are GPS-enabled with trained attendants. Bus fees are separate and depend on the distance from school.

What is the school uniform? +

Our school uniform includes a blue and white combination for regular days and a house-wise sports uniform. Uniforms can be purchased from the school store or authorized vendors. Details are provided at the time of admission.

What are the school timings? +

School timings are from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM for all classes, Monday to Saturday. Saturday is a half-day with special activities. The school remains closed on Sundays and all government holidays.

What documents are required for admission? +

Required documents include: Birth Certificate, Aadhar Card of student and parents, Passport Size Photos (4), Caste Certificate (if applicable), Income Certificate (for scholarship), Transfer Certificate (for classes above Play Group), and Medical Fitness Certificate.
