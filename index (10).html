Welcome to Our School

LAKSHYA CONVENT SCHOOL

Located at Main Road, Jagjivan Nagar, Rajauli (Near Bombay Readymade), Lakshya Convent School is dedicated to nurturing young minds with standard values, disciplined academics, and joy-filled learning from Play School & Nursery up to Class VIII.

Under the visionary leadership of Director Kaushal Kumar Verma and Principal Mrs. Soni Kumari, we create a loving atmosphere where every child's potential is discovered and developed.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality foundational education through modern play-way techniques, disciplined habits, and individual attention.

Our Vision

To shape confident, compassionate, and knowledgeable future citizens equipped with cognitive skills and moral values.

Why Choose Us

Our Premium Facilities

Providing top-tier educational infrastructure and a safe environment for your child's overall growth.

Continuous assessment through weekly tests to monitor learning progress and build exam confidence.

Play Room

Colorful, safe, activity-filled playroom equipped with educational toys for joyful early childhood learning.

Class P-Nur to VIII

Structured curriculum aligned with NCERT standards, covering Play-Nursery to Class 8 systematically.

Big & Green Playground

Spacious outdoor green playground for sports, physical growth, team games, and morning assemblies.

Punctual and safe transport facilities covering Rajauli Main Road and surrounding regional routes.

Music & Dance
