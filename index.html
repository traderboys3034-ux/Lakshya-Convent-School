Why Lakshya Convent School Stands Out in Rajauli

Founded with a vision to bring high-quality, English-medium education accessible to the children of Rajauli and surrounding rural areas, Lakshya Convent School combines academic rigor with holistic personal development.

CBSE Curriculum Pattern

Structured syllabus with strong emphasis on English speaking, Mathematics, Science, and Hindi.

Smart Classroom Tech

Audio-visual animated learning modules that make complex concepts easy and enjoyable for children.

Practical Science & Computer

Practical experiment labs and desktop computer training to build futuristic technical skills.

Discipline & Moral Ethics

Daily morning assembly, yoga, cultural activities, and character building activities.
