Discover our School

Nurturing Young Minds For A Better Tomorrow

At Lakshya Convent School, we believe that education is the key to unlocking a child's true potential. Nestled in the serene locality of Rajauli, Nawada, we offer a rich, safe, and academically stimulating learning environment. With our motto "The Aim of Life with Playschool", we blend early interactive learning with robust secondary foundations up to Class VIII.

Our approach centers on modern educational methodologies, strict self-discipline, artistic creativity, and holistic physical development. We prepare students not just to score in tests, but to think critically and become responsible global citizens.

Premium Facilities Offered

We ensure top-tier facilities for the comfortable mental & physical growth of our young learners.

Smart Classrooms

Equipped with digital visual boards, dynamic audio, and customized slide displays for immersive conceptual understanding.

Play School

Dedicated sensory playzones with educational toys and color blocks, nurturing critical early tactile curiosity.

Computer Lab

High-performance desktop clusters where children master fundamental typing, drawing, and early logic building.

Art & Craft

Creative studios promoting hand-eye coordination with clay crafts, paint canvases, and vibrant paper folding workshops.

Music & Dance

Professional vocal coaching, stage confidence prep, acoustic instruments, and folk/classical dance instruction.

School Bus Service

CCTV Surveillance
